Welcome to প্রদ্যোত (Proddot), the official publication of MGBSDC. This magazine is a celebration of the art of debate, the power of words, and the voices that shape our community.

Featuring in-depth articles, interviews with our brightest debaters, event highlights, and thought-provoking pieces on rhetoric, argumentation, and public speaking. Proddot captures the essence of our club's journey and celebrates the achievements of our members.
