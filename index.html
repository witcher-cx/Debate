Our Story

The Legacy of Excellence

Since 1996, MGBSDC, one of the first debating clubs in history, has been the cornerstone of debating excellence in Bangladesh, nurturing critical thinkers and eloquent speakers who shape the future.

Our club has organized 18 national championships, produced countless champions, and created a legacy that continues to inspire generations. We are more than a club. We are a movement, a family, and a force for intellectual growth.
